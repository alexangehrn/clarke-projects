Privacy Policy

Effective date: [publication date]

Clarke Projects is the trading name of Benjamin Clarke, a sole proprietor based in New York, United States. This policy explains how we handle personal information through this website and in initial enquiries.

For privacy questions or requests, contact benjamin@clarkeprojects.com.

Information we collect

When you submit the contact form, we receive your name, email address, message and the address of the page from which you submitted it. The page address excludes query parameters and URL fragments.

If you contact us directly by email or telephone, we receive the information you choose to provide.

Our hosting provider also processes technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP addresses, browser and device information, requested pages, request times and response statuses.

The form uses technical checks to reduce spam and duplicate submissions, including an email-domain lookup, submission timing and a randomly generated submission identifier. The domain lookup uses only the portion of your email address after the “@”. The timing value is not included in the enquiry email or deliberately retained by our application.

Please do not include financial account details, health information or other sensitive personal information in your enquiry.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate about our services.
  • Maintain relevant correspondence and business records.
  • Operate the website, deliver messages and prevent spam, abuse and duplicate submissions.
  • Meet legal obligations and establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary.

We do not sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising or add people who contact us to marketing lists.

If an enquiry develops into a client engagement, additional information and recordkeeping may be necessary. We will provide further privacy information where required.

Cookies, tracking and external links

This website does not use cookies, browser storage, visitor analytics, advertising trackers or embedded social-media content. We do not track visitors across other websites or permit third-party advertising trackers on this website.

Our Instagram and LinkedIn links are ordinary external links. If you follow them, your interaction with those services is governed by their own privacy policies.

We do not change the website’s behavior in response to browser “Do Not Track” signals because we do not conduct cross-site tracking.

We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, whether or not your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal.

Who receives information

Contact-form enquiries are delivered to Benjamin Clarke’s business email account. We use the following providers to operate the website and deliver and store correspondence:

  • Vercel: website hosting and contact-form processing.
  • Resend: delivery of contact-form emails.
  • Google Workspace: business email hosting and storage.

These providers and their service providers process information as needed to provide, secure and maintain their services.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or legal process, protect someone’s safety, or establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

How long we keep information

For enquiries that do not become client work, we retain correspondence for no more than 24 months after the last exchange, unless particular records need to be kept longer to meet a legal obligation or address a dispute or legal claim.

If an enquiry becomes client work, relevant correspondence becomes part of the client records. Its retention depends on the engagement, applicable recordkeeping obligations and any need to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The 24-month enquiry limit does not apply to those client records.

Resend ordinarily retains email content and delivery logs for 30 days under our current plan. Backup copies are subject to a separate retention cycle.

Hosting, security and other provider-held operational records are subject to the relevant provider’s retention arrangements. Deletion from our active mailbox does not necessarily remove provider-held backup copies immediately.

Your privacy requests

You can contact benjamin@clarkeprojects.com to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold about you.

Depending on the law applicable to your information, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, receive a portable copy of certain information, or complain to a data-protection authority.

We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will respond within the time required by applicable law or, where no specific deadline applies, within a reasonable time. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations or claims; where appropriate, we will explain any limitation on your request.

International processing

Clarke Projects is based in the United States. Information is processed in the United States and may also be processed in other countries where our service providers operate.

Those countries may have different data-protection laws from your country. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we will use the safeguards required for that transfer.

Security

The website uses HTTPS to encrypt information transmitted between your browser and the website. Access to the business mailbox is restricted to Benjamin Clarke, while our providers process information as described above.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This website is intended for adults interested in our professional services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps to delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates on this page and revise the effective date. If a change requires additional notice under applicable law, we will provide that notice.
