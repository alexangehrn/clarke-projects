Effective [date]

This policy explains what personal information Clarke Projects handles through this website, why, and the choices you have. Clarke Projects is [legal name of the business], based in New York. You can reach us about privacy at [email]@clarkeprojects.com.

What we collect

  • When you use the contact form: your name, your email address, your message, and the address of the page you sent it from.
  • When you email or call us directly: whatever you choose to include.
  • When you visit the site: our hosting provider automatically processes technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time.

Please don’t send sensitive information (for example financial account or health details) through the form.

The site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don’t build profiles of visitors. Links to Instagram and LinkedIn are plain links; those services receive information only if you follow them, under their own privacy policies.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and to continue any conversation or work that follows from it.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence.
  • To protect the form from spam and abuse. Before an enquiry is sent, the site checks that your email address’s domain can receive email (a standard public lookup of the domain only) and how long the form was open. Neither is stored.

We don’t sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it for marketing without asking you.

Who receives it

We use a small number of service providers, who handle information on our behalf:

  • Vercel Inc. (United States) hosts the website and runs the contact form.
  • Resend (United States) delivers form enquiries to our mailbox.
  • Google (Google Workspace) provides our email.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

Enquiries stay in our mailbox for as long as needed to respond and to keep business records, and no longer than [retention period], unless they become part of ongoing work. Resend keeps a delivery record and copy of each form message for up to 30 days. Vercel keeps short-lived technical logs under its own retention settings.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, and to correct or delete it, by writing to [email]@clarkeprojects.com. We will reply within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and we will honour them where they apply.

Because the site doesn’t track visitors across other websites, browser “Do Not Track” and Global Privacy Control signals don’t change how it works: it behaves the same for everyone.

Where it is processed

Clarke Projects and its service providers are in the United States. If you contact us from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States.

Security

The site is served only over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to enquiries is limited to the people who need them to reply. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable care to protect what you send us.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
